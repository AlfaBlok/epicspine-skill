How it works

Five steps, run for you, every week.

Each campaign sells one product to one audience. What each call teaches goes into the next shortlist.

  1. ShortlistEvery business that fits your audience, each with the public source that shows it.
  2. First touchA short email, or a posted letter where email is not allowed, with your own phone number on it.
  3. The callWhen they call, our voice agent answers, explains your offer and asks what they need.
  4. The leadThe ones who want more get a meeting in your calendar. You see what they said.
  5. The personOne record per person, so the next touch starts where the last one ended.

The first touch

It follows each country's rules.

Some countries allow a first email to a company. Others do not. Epic Leads picks the channel the law allows.

Where email to companies is allowed

An email

  • The Netherlands, Sweden, Norway, Finland
  • Sent to the company address, with a way to opt out
  • Your phone number and a page about your offer

Where a first email is barred

A posted letter

  • Spain and Denmark
  • Printed and posted to the business address
  • A short code on the letter tells us who called

The call

Every call is answered, day or night.

Your number rings through to our voice agent. It knows your offer, answers questions in the caller's language and books a meeting with the ones who are ready.

You read every call afterwards: who called, what they asked, what was agreed.
