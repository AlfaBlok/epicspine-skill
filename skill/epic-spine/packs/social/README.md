# Pack: social

A social campaign desk: personas scan, write, post, reply and measure; the owner (Jordi) steers by a brief, directions, ideas and after-the-fact feedback. **No global approval gate**: a draft that passes its checks is scheduled and posted in its slot; only the owner's own hold rules hold a post (the rule `Kind: post` holds every post: approve-before-posting, below). Opt in: `Pack: social` in the repo's AGENTS.md. Machine-read source: [`schema.json`](schema.json) (this page summarises it; the schema wins).

## Benefits home (agent_team E71-S2, approved under E71-A4 2026-11-07)

The brief opens with `## Benefits`: what the product does for the user (`What it does`, plain words) paired with what the user gets (`Benefit`), each with a status and the real screen that shows it (agent_team E71-S2 iteration 2). Every persona's wake text carries it as a key element (`pack.py social --wake-text --role R`, from the schema's `benefits` key). The rules:

- **Posts.** A new post shows one benefit from the table, names its number in a `Benefit` column (optional: a ledger without it reads as empty) and opens on that benefit, with the real screen as proof, then may ask a question. Check `benefit_named` makes a draft with no benefit, or with one that is not `works today`, stay a draft; a half built or not built line is only ever called coming soon. A repo with no Benefits section is not asked, and rows already approved or scheduled are never rewritten to fit.
- **Ideas.** An idea may come without a benefit; the Writer fills the optional `Benefit` column when it takes the idea. One benefit fans out into many ideas and posts across platforms.
- **Writing a benefit** (the recipe, EPIC-71 L-1): draft first, five lines or fewer on one screen, before any research; if the voice matters and there is no example, ask for one or write one and say so; walk the product in the order the user meets it, one thing per line; each line pairs What it does (plain words: it writes, it holds, it answers) with the Benefit it brings (what the user gets, in their terms); only this product, never another one the team posts for; no slogans, abstract nouns or method names; mark each line works today, half built or not built, with a real screen.
- **When Jordi gives his own lines** ("here is an example, WDYT?"; EPIC-71 L-2): his words are the floor and the voice sample, never the deliverable. Write for the end user; show his version beside ours line by line with each change named; add what his lines miss and one bolder option; read them to a cold reader first; after his approve, ask for his verdict on the result. Why: in the benefits rounds his seven lines came back unchanged, approved, and he said 52 minutes later that we had added nothing.
- **Discovery.** What people say they want is noted with its link as an Observations line ending `proposed for the brief (Benefits): "<line>"` once two notes agree; Jordi adds it to the table or says not now. Only Jordi edits the table.

## Product deck and metrics (agent_team E78-S1, Jordi's voice note 2026-11-08)

- **Product deck, `data/product.md`**: what we sell, one `## ` part per slide in Jordi's order: Why it exists, Who it is for, How it works, What it does for you, What is included, How much it costs, How you get it, How it differs (schema `product`; `pack.py init` writes the file with these headings when it is missing). It is the landing page as a deck, separate from each campaign's deck (its spine's Brief). It links, never copies: benefit lines stay in `data/brief.md` Benefits. Jordi and the Delivery manager write it; every persona reads it at each wake and never promises more than it says. **Each campaign's product deck, `docs/campaigns/<epic-n>/product.md`** (agent_team E78-S1 round 2): what that campaign pushes (product, service or promo), one part per slide: What we push, Who it is for, What they get, The offer, How they get it, What we say and never say (schema `product.campaign_file`, `campaign_parts`). It narrows the project's deck, never contradicts it. **Read order** (`product.read_order`, carried in every role's wake text): the project's product deck, then the campaign's, then `data/metrics.md` (the targets and the role's line). **What Jordi said** (`product.jordi_part`, agent_team EPIC-81): both decks end with this part, the lines of his voice notes worth keeping about that product or campaign, newest first, one line each `- **<d Mon YYYY>:** "<his words>" ([voice note HH:MM UTC](<Zenod link>))`, quoted as transcribed, never a paraphrase, never invented. The day after a note the host's voice-note reader appends them (agent_team: the Input reviewer's daily run), one commit per repo; another world's decks get a desk note. Every persona reads the part first at each wake and raises a change it asks for in Observations (`proposed for the product deck`), never edits the other parts on it.
- **Metrics catalog** (schema `metrics`): one list of numbers a campaign may target: testers, signups, site_visitors, code_views, x_followers, reddit_followers, posts_out, replies_received, backlinks (sites our visitors came from and GitHub's referring sites, ours and search engines left out), mcp_listings (the world's own `data/mcp_listings.md`, one row per catalog that lists it live) and customers (tester records marked paid, not cancelled; agent_team E78-S8). A campaign names its target in its spine's `## Campaign plan` with one line `Metric: <id> <target> by <YYYY-MM-DD>` (`from <date>` optional, else the `Period:` start; a line Jordi has not set ends `(proposed: Jordi sets it)`). **Sources per world** (schema `metrics.sources`, agent_team E78-S9): each number reads its world's own source, never another world's. A world sets them with one line in its root spine, `Metric sources: testers ledger, site_visitors none`; a campaign overrides with `source <id>` at the end of its Metric line. Sources: `default` (the catalog's own: the host's testers form, counter, GitHub traffic, ledgers), `ledger` (the world's own `data/<id>.md`, one table row per unit, a `Date` column counts the period; for numbers only that world sees, like Zenod testers who come by DM or cloud.zenod.dev sign-ups), `none` (no source yet), and `zenod-counts#<field>` (agent_team E80-S4: Zenod's public counts at https://cloud.zenod.dev/api/public/counts, read by the host at most once an hour, fields `applications`, `accepted`, `signed_up`, `active_testers`, `paying_customers`; a cumulative field over a campaign's period counts what came after its start; the number shows where it came from and links Zenod's testers and counts page; when the endpoint does not answer the last good reading stays and says so). Only named sources are read, never an address a spine writes: `url:https://cloud.zenod.dev/api/public/counts#<field>` is taken as the same source.
- **`data/metrics.md`**: written by the host (the agent_team console) at its hourly check, only when a number changed: each campaign's target and where it stands, the catalog with today's values, and one row per role (the number it wakes to move, how, and the files it reads). Never edited by hand; every persona reads it at each wake.
- **Daily log** (agent_team E82-S1, Jordi's voice note 2026-11-08: "track … what types of posts are we making, big decisions or big changes in tone … for one month"; schema `daily_log`): a campaign whose `## Campaign plan` has a `Daily log: <link>` line keeps one table, `docs/campaigns/<epic-n>/daily-log.md`, one row per day with no gap: Day, Date, Posts (with links), Replies sent, Decisions, Tone changes, Followers, Impressions, Likes, Replies received, Note; below it one line per decision (`D-<n>`) or tone change (`T-<n>`) with who, why and a link, the day it happens. The Reporter fills the day's row at its daily wake with numbers from the Team Browser's read tools (social_stats, x_my_posts; 48 h numbers corrected in place, marked `48h`; a failed read leaves the cell empty and says why in Note, never a guess). The Writer, Publisher and Responder add their post ids and replies in the wake that makes them; anyone who changes tone or plan adds its line. It feeds the campaign's report and, at its end, the case study.
- **Caps**: X takes up to 3 posts a day (`threads_per_day_by_platform`), Reddit keeps 1 a day and 1 a week per community The weekly cap per community applies only on the platforms in `per_community_per_week_platforms` (default `["reddit"]`, where a community is a subreddit); X's Community column holds the account handle, so X is never capped by it (agent_team E78-S5). The account's own Reddit profile (Community `u/jordimr`, posted to the subreddit `u_jordimr`) is not a community: a campaign whose brief (`## Campaign plan`) has a line naming `u/<handle>` and the word profile gets one profile post a day there (`profile_posts_per_day`), still inside Reddit's 1 a day; any other campaign keeps 1 a week there (agent_team E60-S9).

## Post statuses

`idea → draft → scheduled → posted → measured`, plus `held`, `pulled`, `expired`. `idea` is the ideas.md row; the ledger row is written at `draft`.

| From | To | Who | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| idea | draft | Writer | takes the idea |
| draft | scheduled | Writer | every check passes, no hold rule matches |
| draft | held | Writer | a hold rule matches |
| held | scheduled | Jordi | approves it (the console writes `Approved <date>` in the row's Feedback) |
| held | scheduled | Writer | Jordi approved it with feedback (`Approved with feedback <date>: …` in Feedback): the Writer applies it to the text, then schedules it |
| scheduled | posted | Publisher | its slot; link in the same commit |
| scheduled | expired | Publisher | best-before passed |
| scheduled | held | Publisher | its slot passed over 24 h ago (the console moves such a post to its next free slot first, agent_team E34-S32) and Jordi has not released it since (his `Approved` or `Post now` note dated at or after the slot): a late post never goes out, it waits for Jordi (rule `no_late_post`, agent_team E60-S2) |
| draft | posted | Responder, Engager | reply / outreach rows, checks pass |
| posted | measured | Reporter (any); Responder, Engager (their rows) | 48 h after posting |
| draft, held, scheduled | pulled | Jordi | unscheduled, never goes out |
| scheduled | held | Jordi | Restore of a retired campaign: its posts whose slot passed while it was retired come back held, for him to post now or give a new slot (agent_team E60-S2) |
| posted, measured | pulled | Jordi | the Publisher deletes the live post at once, link kept (C5) |
| pulled | held, scheduled, draft | Jordi | Undo of a pull (restore) on a post that never went out (no Link): back to the status it had before, with its slot; a scheduled post whose slot has passed comes back `held`, for him to post now or give a new slot |

A failed check keeps the draft a draft, with the reason in `checks`.

**Ideas never expire (agent_team E64-S4).** Ideas: `new → open → spent`, or `parked` (why; back to open) or `epic` (too big for one post), all moved by the Writer; Jordi may drop an open idea (`new`, `open`, or legacy `picked up`, `drafted`, `posted` → `parked`, the console's drop). An idea dropped for good goes `rejected` (from `new`, `open` or `parked`, by Jordi or the Writer recording his drop): the pile of what we dropped, its Why keeping the lesson and who dropped it; only Jordi takes it back to `open`. An idea goes `open` with its first post and stays open: the Writer picks an open idea and a platform it has not been used on (`used on`, computed from the posts' Idea and Platform), so one idea becomes a Reddit thread, an X post and later a LinkedIn post, each written fresh; a post's Idea may repeat across rows. It closes only when Jordi parks it or the Writer marks it `spent` (every platform it fits is used, or the angle is stale; Why says which). `picked up`, `drafted` and `posted` are legacy states (read as open; the Writer moves them to `open`); their old moves carry `"legacy": true`: the validator still accepts them, role texts no longer list them.

**Thread replies wait for Jordi (agent_team E64-S6).** An outreach row (a disclosed reply in someone else's thread) matching a hold rule (the brief's `Kind: outreach`) goes `draft → held` by the Engager, never straight to `posted` (check `hold_rules_outreach`); Jordi's Approve moves it `held → scheduled` (his `Approved` note; with feedback the Engager applies it first); the Engager sends it inside the caps (`scheduled → posted`, link in the same commit) or, when it waited more than 12 h on X or 24 h on Reddit, marks it `expired` (cooled), never sent late.

**Campaigns inside a repo (optional `Campaign` column, agent_team E60-S1).** A campaign is a child epic of the social repo (`docs/spines/*.md` with a `## Campaign plan` section: period, platform, key messages, voice, posting and reply style, targets, personas and cadence). A post, reply or outreach row counts toward it when its `Campaign` cell holds the campaign's epic code (e.g. `EPIC-7`); the code never changes when the campaign is renamed. Empty, or a ledger without the column, means the repo itself (no campaign), so older ledgers stay valid as they are. Writer, Responder and Engager set it on the rows they add for a campaign; Jordi may retag a row.

**Approve before posting (opt-in per campaign, agent_team E46-S2).** The pack keeps no global gate; a campaign that wants Jordi to approve every post adds the hold rule `Kind: post` to its brief's Hold for me table (the console's "Approve every post" switch writes it). **Per campaign (agent_team E74-S5):** a repo holding several campaigns gives each its own switch: `Kind: post, Campaign: <its code>` holds only that campaign's posts (`Campaign: none`: rows with no Campaign, the repo level); the plain `Kind: post` still covers every campaign without one. A new campaign starts with its own rule (auto-approve off until Jordi switches it on). Every new post then goes `draft → held`; Jordi approves (→ `scheduled`), asks for a change (`Revise requested <date>: …` in Feedback: the Writer makes a new version, keeps it `held` and starts Checks with `Revised <date>`, so it comes back to him for review; it is never scheduled without his `Approved` note), approves with feedback (stays `held` until the Writer applies it and schedules it) or does not post it (→ `pulled`; restore puts a never-posted pulled post back to the status it had before the pull: `held`, or `scheduled` with its slot while that slot is ahead, else `held`). The `approved` check makes `held → scheduled` need Jordi's `Approved` note in Feedback, a column only Jordi writes.

## Messages: DMs wait for Jordi's Send (agent_team E34-S27)

DMs are not posts: they never go to the posts ledger (the hook refuses a new posts row whose Community is `DM …` or `DMs`). Each conversation is a Chats row; each message a Messages row.

| Message status | To | Who | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| (new) | received | Responder | their message, as it came |
| (new) | draft | Responder | our reply, for Jordi's Send |
| draft | scheduled | **Jordi only** | Send: his `Approved <date>` note in Feedback (`send_approved`) |
| draft | pulled | Jordi | Don't send; Undo: pulled → draft |
| scheduled | draft | Jordi | Undo of Send before it went out |
| scheduled | posted | Responder | sends it exactly as written, dry run first; the send in Result (`sent`); Text is frozen once scheduled |
| draft | expired | Responder | they wrote again first; a new draft answers them |

The message lifecycle declares its own `approval_gate: true`, so the pack's "no global gate" stays true for posts. Chat states: `open` (they wrote last), `waiting` (we did), `left` (by rule: Reason + Link, one row per chat, never a bundle), `hidden` (the request text is hidden until accepted; the Responder never accepts on its own), `done`. Jordi's buttons: Draft a reply for me (left → open, `Draft requested`), Accept and draft a reply (hidden → open, `Accept requested`), Done, no reply needed (→ done, Undo back). Moving old DM rows out of the posts ledger: the Delivery manager adds them here and sets the old rows to `moved` (Result `moved to data/messages-ledger.md C-…`), never deletes them; the export skips `moved` rows.

## Checks (validator, E34-S2)

At `draft → scheduled` / `draft → posted` (the text checks also at `draft → held` and `held → scheduled`): Reddit no link on line 1 and at most one link; disclosure ("I built…") in the first two lines on Reddit for posts and outreach (a reply in our own thread needs none); no Never say phrase; caps (1 thread a day, 1 per community a week, 20 replies a day); hold rules (→ held). At `held → scheduled`: Jordi's `Approved` note in Feedback (`approved`). At `scheduled → posted` / `draft → posted`: the link is in the row. Messages: `send_approved` at `draft → scheduled` (Jordi's `Approved`), `sent` at `→ posted` (Result filled), never-say at Send; checks name the lifecycles they apply to (`lifecycles`). Every status change must be a listed transition by one of its roles (the role is checked when the commit names its writer, `EPICSPINE_PERSONA`). How to run it: [references/packs.md](../../references/packs.md) Validate.

## Desk intake (6 kinds)

What Jordi says in the desk chat is one of six kinds; the desk writes it where the kind says (as Jordi, through the console's write API) and replies with where it landed (file + section link) and undo. Brief change → brief sections (root Goal/Targets, brief.md); Key message → brief.md Points to emphasize (+ weight); Priority → brief.md Week / Month / This week only; Idea → ideas.md row (from: Jordi, state new, + assets); Big idea → a new child epic (Delivery manager); Feedback on a post → the post's Feedback + message house Feedback log. Unsure: ask one line back. Source: `schema.json` `intake`; prompt text: `pack.py social --wake-text` (a persona's own: `--role Writer`; [references/packs.md](../../references/packs.md) Desk intake).

**Kinds.** An ongoing campaign (e.g. X awareness) is `Spine kind: standing`: weekly `## Targets`, a `Scoreboard:` line, never `done`; a missed target is a task row. A campaign with an end (a one-week push, recruiting N testers) is an ordinary epic that closes with its `## Outcome`.

Daily rounds stay the plan's `### Personas and cadence`, not rows. A row whose outcome needs Jordi goes to `review` with its card and shows on his Team home and first on the campaign's Work tab.
