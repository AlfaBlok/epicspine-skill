Why EpicTeam exists: from one prompt at a time to one objective over time.

Our vision in seven frames. Each makes one point, and together they tell the story.

  1. 1 of 7

    AI is the technology of our lifetime.

    It is on the scale of discovering fire, and its power only grows from here. Yet we still reach it in a very limited way.

    What limits us now is how we use it.

  2. 2 of 7

    We still use it one prompt at a time.

    You ask, it answers, and it stops. A prompt is one snapshot: one question at one moment.

    Question and answer is the bottleneck.

  3. 3 of 7

    Now set one objective, and keep it over time.

    An objective is ongoing. Hundreds of prompts serve it, each one changed to fit the moment, until its key results turn green.

    You move up from operator to decision maker.

  4. 4 of 7

    One person can follow about twenty threads.

    Each thread runs five to twenty minutes and comes back when it is done. Switching between them wears you out, and the deep thinking gets lost.

    The limit is human attention, so the agents need a shared aim.

  5. 5 of 7

    A thousand lanterns, pointed at one spot.

    Start many agents with no shared aim and their light scatters. Point every one at the same objective and the light becomes fire.

    EpicTeam concentrates every agent's work on your objective.

  6. 6 of 7

    Agents that chase a green scorecard.

    Companies already work to turn their scorecards green. Here the agents do the chasing. You stay in the loop with a voice note, even from a walk.

    The discipline makes the progress compound.

  7. 7 of 7

    Why does one person run one AI subscription, and not a hundred?

    Because there was no way to steer more. We steer sixteen today. With clear direction and good taste, one person could steer a hundred, all working in parallel.

    Soon each person sets an intelligence budget and shares it across objectives.
