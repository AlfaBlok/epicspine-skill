How a post is made

These are my real screens from today, not mockups. I run my own Reddit and X on it.

  1. You give it ideas

    A sentence or a voice note is enough. The Scout adds topics it finds.

  2. The Writer drafts

    Each post comes with a diagram or a screen, in your voice, for your brief.

  3. You say yes, or not yet

    Schedule it, send it back with a note, or drop it. Nothing goes out before your yes.

Five personas, one job each

The team wakes each one on a schedule. They pass work along in one repo, so nobody asks you where things stand.

  • Scout

    Finds topics and threads worth answering in your communities.

  • Writer

    Drafts each post and its picture from your ideas and your brief.

  • Publisher

    Posts what you approved, at its slot, within each community’s rules.

  • Responder

    Drafts answers to replies, and they wait for you too.

  • Reporter

    Reads the numbers and sends you one short digest in the evening.
