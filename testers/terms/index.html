The tester contract

EpicSocial is free for 5 teams. Here is what we give, what we ask back, and four rules.

Apply to test it What EpicSocial does

We give you

EpicSocial and Jordi

  • Your own agent team for your Reddit, your X, or both.
  • A private space that only you log into.
  • Your brief, drafted from your application. You correct it in the app.
  • Four weeks at no cost. No card, and nothing renews.
  • Every voice note you send gets read and acted on.

You give us

Your team, for four weeks

  • Your team set up within your first five days.
  • One voice note a week, from the app, while you use it.
  • Ideas when you have them. A sentence or a voice note is enough.
  • A yes or a note on each draft, ideally within a day.
  • A look at what your team does, so we can learn from it.

The rules

  1. 1

    Set up within five days

    Your five days start when we send your login. By day five, your brief is done, a few ideas are in, and your first draft is judged. If not, we email you once. On day six your seat goes to the next team.

  2. 2

    One voice note a week

    Your login comes with a voice note link. Once a week, talk for a minute about what worked and what got in your way. We only transcribe it.

  3. 3

    Stay inside the limits

    Your team’s own schedule never counts. What you ask for on top refills every five hours.

    40messages to your team per window
    10extra runs you ask for per window
    3posts a day on each channel
  4. 4

    Five places, picked on quality

    We pick the 5 strongest applications, not the first 5. After that the form stays open as a waitlist, and a freed seat goes to the next team on it.

Voice first. No calls.

You never have to book a call with us. Whenever you have feedback, leave a voice note.

Nobody listens to your voice. The recording is transcribed, and your team’s agents read the text and sum it up for Jordi.

How to apply well

  • You already post on Reddit or X, and you can show us where.
  • You have real users or a launch date.
  • One person on your team will judge drafts every day.
  • You can say in three lines what you want your team to post about.

A one-line application goes to the bottom of the list. Jordi reads every application and emails you either way.
