EpicSocial Apply

Free for 5 teams. Use it in your first five days, or your seat goes to the next team.

EpicSocial gives you a social team of agents for your Reddit and X. We want 5 teams who will really use it, and we will see everything they do.

The five-day rule. If you're not using the product within your first five days, we give your seat to someone else.

Your five days start when we send your login. By day five your team should be running: your brief written, a few ideas in, and your first draft approved or sent back with a note. If it isn't, we email you once. On day six your seat goes to the next team on the list.

What you get

  • Your own team of six personas: the Scout, the Writer, the Publisher, the Responder, the Engager and the Reporter.
  • A private space that only you log into, for your Reddit, your X, or both.
  • Your brief, written with us on a short call.
  • A direct line to Jordi while things are rough.
  • Four weeks at no cost. You give no card, and nothing renews.

What you can use

Your personas run on their own schedule, and that never counts against you. What you ask for on top, chatting with your team or asking for an extra run, comes from an allowance that refills every five hours.

40messages to your team in each five-hour window
10extra runs you ask for in each five-hour window
3posts a day on each channel, inside each community's own rules

When you reach a limit, your team waits for the next window. Nothing is charged and nothing is lost.

What we ask of you

  • Let us see everything your team does: its runs, its drafts and your feedback. That is how we learn.
  • Send a few ideas a week. A sentence or a voice note is enough.
  • Approve or comment on drafts, ideally within a day.
  • Send a few lines of feedback each week, or take a 15-minute call.

How to apply well

We pick the 5 strongest applications, not the first 5. These are the things we look for:

  • You already post on Reddit or X, and you can show us where.
  • You have a product or project with real users or a launch date.
  • One person on your team will approve drafts every day.
  • You can say in three lines what you want your team to post about.

A one-line application goes to the bottom of the list. Jordi reads every application and emails you either way.

When the 5 places are taken

The form stays open and the waitlist starts. A seat that isn't used in its first five days goes to the next team on the list, so a strong application on the waitlist can still get in this month.
